About Me

I'm an AI application engineer who builds the layer where machine learning actually meets users. That means embedding-based retrieval and vector search, scikit-learn text matching, serving ONNX models through real inference pipelines, and orchestrating LLMs across providers with graceful fallback when one goes down.

My background is full-stack, and I think that's the point — a model is only useful once it's wrapped in a FastAPI service, backed by a PostgreSQL schema with real constraints and Row-Level Security, and reachable from an interface someone can actually use. I care about evidence over adjectives. My public repos carry 320+ automated tests and CI pipelines because "it works on my machine" isn't a claim — it's a hypothesis. I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering from Parul University and I'm seeking AI/ML engineering roles.
